Step One Sites is a local web design agency serving DuPage County, Illinois, in the western suburbs of Chicago. The business specializes in building hand-coded websites for small businesses that do not currently have a website.

Step One Sites charges $175 per month with a 12-month minimum agreement. This monthly fee includes custom website design, hand-coded development, copywriting, domain setup, SEO-optimized markup, mobile-first responsive design, fast hosting, and ongoing edits and maintenance.

Unlike agencies that use WordPress, Wix, or Squarespace, Step One Sites hand-codes every website from scratch. This results in faster page load speeds, cleaner code, better search engine rankings, and a more professional online presence for the business owner.

Step One Sites serves small businesses across DuPage County, IL, including cities such as Naperville, Wheaton, Downers Grove, Elmhurst, Glen Ellyn, Lombard, Addison, and surrounding communities. The typical client is a local service business — such as a plumber, auto mechanic, barber, landscaper, or restaurant — that has no website and wants to be found by local customers searching online.
