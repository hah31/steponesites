Frequently Asked Questions

Do you use WordPress or Wix?

No. Every site we build is hand-coded from scratch using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. This means faster load times, cleaner code, better SEO, and no bloated plugins or security vulnerabilities that come with WordPress and Wix.

What if I want to cancel early?

The 12-month agreement ensures we can invest the time to build you a quality site. If you need to cancel early, the remaining months are due. After your initial 12 months, you can cancel anytime with 30 days' notice.

Do I own my website?

Yes. You own all the content on your website. If you cancel after your agreement, we'll hand over the complete source code. Your domain is registered in your name from day one.

What does the 12-month agreement cover?

Everything: custom website design and development, copywriting, domain setup, SEO-optimized markup, fast hosting, and ongoing edits and maintenance. There are no hidden fees or surprise charges.

How long does it take to launch?

Most sites go live within two weeks of signing up. Timelines can vary if we're waiting on content or information from you, but we move fast and keep you updated every step of the way.

Is the domain included?

We help you choose and set up your domain. The domain registration fee (typically around $12/year) is separate, but we handle the entire setup process for you so you don't have to deal with the technical side.

What if I need something beyond a basic site?

Our standard plan covers a professional multi-page website. If you need e-commerce, booking systems, or a custom web application, we can discuss a custom scope and pricing. Just ask — we're happy to talk through what you need.
