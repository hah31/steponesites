About Ochoa's Outdoor Services

Founded in 2017 by Ariel Ochoa, we've been transforming outdoor spaces across the western Chicago suburbs with dedication, craftsmanship, and a passion for the outdoors.

What started as a small landscaping operation in Bensenville has grown into a full-service outdoor company trusted by homeowners and businesses alike. We take pride in every project — whether it's a simple lawn cleanup or a complete hardscaping transformation.

Our team is known for being responsive, thorough, and easy to work with. We treat every property like our own and stand behind the quality of our work.
